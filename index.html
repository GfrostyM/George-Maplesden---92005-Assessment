What is Biodiversity?

Biodiversity in basic terms, is the variety of all life on Earth. This includes all living organisms such as animals, plants and other life.

Biodiversity loss in New Zealand

New Zealand/Aotearoa is a very biodiverse country as a whole. There are large areas of bush and forrest which contain various native birds plants and much more. In New Zealand over many years our biodiversity has been on the decline. Since human arrival, at least 75 animal and plant species have gone extinct and many more are at risk.

What are the main problems?

There are a couple of primary reasons as to why biodiversity loss has been occurring. The first reason is pests, there are hunting and killing lots of our native wildlife. Secondly there is deforestation, humans cutting down trees and destroying land is another cause of biodiversity loss.
